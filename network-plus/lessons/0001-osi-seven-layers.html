OSI: seven layers, one clue each

Win: given a symptom or a clue (MAC, IP, port, cable), name the layer in under 5 seconds. Troubleshooting (24%) and concepts questions both lean on this.

Learn: layer = clue

#LayerClueUnit
7ApplicationWhat you see: HTTP, DNS, POP3Data
6PresentationEncoding, SSL/TLS encryptionData
5SessionStart/stop/restart conversations, tunnelingData
4TransportTCP/UDP, port numbers, segmentationSegment / Datagram
3NetworkIP addresses, routers, fragmentationPacket
2Data LinkMAC addresses, switchesFrame
1PhysicalCable, fiber, wireless signalBits
Mnemonic (bottom-up): Please Do Not Teach Students Pointless Abbreviations.

OSI is a common language, not the protocol suite in use; real traffic is TCP/IP, mapped onto these layers. [notes/1.1-osi-model.md]
