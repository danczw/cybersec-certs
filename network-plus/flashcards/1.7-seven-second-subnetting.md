# Seven Second Subnetting Flashcards

**Q:** What makes seven second subnetting different from the magic number method?
**A:** No math involved — all values are predefined in a chart; eliminates the 256 − mask calculation
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** subnetting, seven-second, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the only arithmetic required in seven second subnetting?
**A:** Adding 1 (for first IP) and subtracting 1 (for last IP)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** subnetting, seven-second, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the four steps of seven second subnetting?
**A:** 1) Convert CIDR to decimal subnet mask (use chart) 2) Determine subnet/network address 3) Determine broadcast address 4) Calculate first IP (subnet + 1) and last IP (broadcast − 1)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** subnetting, seven-second, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In the seven second subnetting chart, how do you build the Networks column?
**A:** Start at 2, double down (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** subnetting, seven-second, domain-1, prof-messer
