# Hardware Tools Flashcards

**Q:** What is a tone generator used for?
**A:** To find a single cable in a large bunch of cables — puts an analog tone on the wire
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** tone-generator, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an inductive probe?
**A:** A device that listens for the tone from a tone generator — only needs to get close to the cable (does not require direct contact)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** tone-generator, inductive-probe, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are four ways to connect a tone generator to a cable?
**A:** Modular jack, coax connection, punchdown block, or alligator clips
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** tone-generator, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What additional function do many tone generator/probe combos provide?
**A:** They also double as a cable tester
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** tone-generator, cable-tester, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a cable tester do?
**A:** Checks continuity from one end of a cable to the other — verifies pin-to-pin connections (pin 1 to pin 1, pin 2 to pin 2, etc.)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** cable-tester, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What three problems can a cable tester identify?
**A:** Pins that are not connected, shorts in the cable, and crossed wires (e.g., pin 1 connects to pin 3)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** cable-tester, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the limitations of a cable tester?
**A:** Simple continuity test only — does not show signal capacity or cable category
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** cable-tester, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does a cable tester indicate correct wiring?
**A:** Lights up pins 1 through 8 in order
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** cable-tester, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a network tap do?
**A:** Intercepts network traffic and sends a copy to a protocol analyzer or packet capture device
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** network-tap, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is a physical tap installed?
**A:** Physically breaks the connection and inserts the tap in the middle — may require downtime to install
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** network-tap, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a passive tap?
**A:** A network tap that is not powered; often used for optical fiber
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** network-tap, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an active tap?
**A:** A network tap that requires additional power to regenerate the signal
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** network-tap, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What connections does a network tap have?
**A:** In/out connections for the network link and separate monitor ports for the analyzer
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** network-tap, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is SPAN (Switched Port Analyzer)?
**A:** A function within the switch itself that copies data from any interface and sends it to a different interface where a protocol analyzer is connected
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** span, port-mirroring, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What advantage does port mirroring/SPAN have over a physical tap?
**A:** No physical break in the connection is needed
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** span, port-mirroring, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the limitation of port mirroring/SPAN?
**A:** Limited bandwidth available — e.g., tapping a 2-Gbps link (1 Gbps each direction) but the mirror port only has 1 Gbps to the analyzer
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** span, port-mirroring, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When should you use a physical tap instead of port mirroring?
**A:** For very large bandwidth uses where port mirroring cannot handle the total traffic
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** span, network-tap, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What do wireless survey tools provide?
**A:** Details about what is going over the air — signal coverage information and identification of potential interference (especially from other access points)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless-survey, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where might wireless survey tools be found?
**A:** Built into the OS or access point, or as a third-party tool
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless-survey, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a Wi-Fi analyzer show?
**A:** Channel strengths, channels in use, access points in the area, and potential sources of interference; sees all 802.11 information going through the air
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wifi-analyzer, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What might a Wi-Fi analyzer require for detailed information?
**A:** Specialized hardware
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wifi-analyzer, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a spectrum analyzer show?
**A:** An entire range of frequencies and every signal broadcast on those frequencies (802.11 or other sources) — can verify interference from third-party equipment or microwave ovens
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wifi-analyzer, spectrum-analyzer, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does signal-to-noise ratio look like in a Wi-Fi analyzer?
**A:** Narrow ratio indicates weak signal relative to noise; wide separation indicates strong signal
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wifi-analyzer, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a visual fault locator?
**A:** A light source specifically designed for optical fiber — used to identify breaks in fiber optics
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** visual-fault-locator, fiber, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does a visual fault locator identify breaks?
**A:** Light leaks out from around a break, making it visible
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** visual-fault-locator, fiber, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What conditions may be needed to see a visual fault locator's results?
**A:** May need to turn off room lights to see clearly
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** visual-fault-locator, fiber, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When can a visual fault locator be used in the installation process?
**A:** Before installing a fiber patch into the network — can locate faults ahead of time
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** visual-fault-locator, fiber, domain-5, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How would you describe a visual fault locator's technology level and effectiveness?
**A:** Low-tech but very effective at finding fiber problems
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** visual-fault-locator, fiber, domain-5, prof-messer
