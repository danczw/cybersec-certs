# Network Documentation Flashcards

**Q:** What does a physical network map show?
**A:** The physical layout of equipment, cabling, and connections — can track cables back to equipment in the room
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, network-maps, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a logical network map show?
**A:** A higher-level view of how the entire network is connected — not focused on individual interfaces or cables
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, network-maps, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When is a logical network map useful?
**A:** When planning additional locations — view the existing WAN layout and decide where to add new installations
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, network-maps, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name three tools for creating network maps.
**A:** Visio, OmniGraffle, Gliffy.com
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, network-maps, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a rack diagram?
**A:** A physical view of a rack as if standing in front of it — shows equipment location by rack unit
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, rack-diagram, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why are rack diagrams useful?
**A:** When data center access is restricted — allows remote guidance (e.g., "go to unit 14 for the power strip")
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, rack-diagram, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What location information can a rack diagram include?
**A:** Row and rack identifier (e.g., row 3, rack W)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, rack-diagram, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a cable map?
**A:** A physical diagram of where wires are installed in a facility
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, cable-map, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where is a cable map usually posted?
**A:** On the wall in the IDF or MDF
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, cable-map, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What do the numbered drops on a cable map represent?
**A:** They correlate desk drops with patch panel connections in the wiring closet
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, cable-map, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When are cable maps useful?
**A:** During installation and troubleshooting — track where a wire was run and correlate with network problems
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, cable-map, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a Layer 1/2/3 network diagram combine?
**A:** Physical (L1: interfaces), data link (L2: MAC addresses), and network (L3: IP addresses) views
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, network-maps, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In a Layer 1/2/3 diagram, what does each layer show?
**A:** L1: physical interfaces where wires connect; L2: MAC addresses; L3: IP addresses associated with each MAC
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** documentation, network-maps, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are asset tags?
**A:** Labels placed on every asset (laptops, desktops, routers, firewalls, switches) for identification and tracking
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name four uses of asset tags.
**A:** Reference in trouble tickets, financial tracking (depreciation), purchase date/warranty tracking, owner identification
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What might an asset tag include besides a label?
**A:** A barcode or RFID
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an asset database?
**A:** A centralized database combining asset tag, device components, and assigned user
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Who uses the asset database?
**A:** Support team, accounting, and finance
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How can you determine a device's physical location from the asset database?
**A:** Find the assigned user — their location is the device's location
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does the asset database help with software licensing?
**A:** Tracks software on devices — determines how many licenses need to be purchased
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, asset-management, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does IPAM stand for?
**A:** IP Address Management
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, ipam, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does an IPAM solution do?
**A:** Plan, track, and configure DHCP and IP address schemes throughout an organization
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, ipam, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is mapping users to IP addresses at specific dates/times useful?
**A:** Because dynamic IP addressing means a user's IP changes — need to know who had which address and when
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, ipam, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What problems can IPAM help identify?
**A:** Shortages or problems with IP address configurations — may lead to modifying ranges or adding addresses to DHCP
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, ipam, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What protocols does IPAM manage?
**A:** Both IPv4 and IPv6 addressing from one central console
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, ipam, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a Service Level Agreement (SLA)?
**A:** A contract defining the minimum level of service required from a provider
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** documentation, sla, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Give two examples of SLA metrics.
**A:** 99.99% uptime requirement; no more than four hours of unscheduled downtime
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, sla, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How might a provider meet an SLA for quick recovery?
**A:** Quickly dispatch a technician or keep spare equipment on-site
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, sla, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a wireless site survey?
**A:** Identifying all access points in use and documenting frequencies to configure systems for minimum interference
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, site-survey, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Does a site survey only cover access points you control?
**A:** No — it identifies all APs including ones not under your control
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, site-survey, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When should site surveys be performed often?
**A:** In multi-company buildings or large campuses with changing wireless environments
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, site-survey, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a heat map in the context of a site survey?
**A:** A document created by walking around with a mobile device to show how wireless signal propagates throughout a building
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** documentation, site-survey, domain-3, prof-messer
