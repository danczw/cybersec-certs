# VPNs Flashcards

**Q:** What is the primary purpose of a VPN?
**A:** Encrypts data that would normally be sent in the clear so it can be sent securely over public networks like the internet
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** vpn, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a VPN concentrator?
**A:** A device that encrypts and decrypts VPN traffic — usually built into modern firewalls
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** vpn, concentrator, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the two forms a VPN concentrator can take?
**A:** Dedicated hardware designed for encryption/decryption, or software installed on an existing server
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, concentrator, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a client-to-site VPN?
**A:** VPN client software installed on a user's workstation that communicates back to a VPN concentrator at a corporate network
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** vpn, client-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the two activation modes for a client-to-site VPN?
**A:** Manually enabled/disabled by the user, or configured as an always-on connection that activates at login
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, client-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Describe the traffic flow for a client-to-site VPN (outbound).
**A:** Client encrypts → sends over internet → concentrator decrypts → forwards to internal network
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, client-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Describe the traffic flow for a client-to-site VPN (return).
**A:** Corporate network → concentrator encrypts → sends over internet → client decrypts
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, client-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a site-to-site VPN?
**A:** A VPN that encrypts all communication between two sites over a tunnel, with concentrators on both ends
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** vpn, site-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is a site-to-site VPN typically configured?
**A:** As an always-on connection built into existing firewalls at each location with the VPN concentrator function enabled on both sides
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, site-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Are users at remote sites aware that a site-to-site VPN is encrypting their traffic?
**A:** No — the concentrators handle encryption/decryption transparently; users have no idea it's occurring
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** vpn, site-to-site, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a clientless VPN?
**A:** A VPN that runs inside a browser using HTML5 without requiring separate VPN client software
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, clientless, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What technology enables clientless VPNs to work within a browser?
**A:** HTML5's Web Cryptography API — provides encrypted tunnel functionality within the browser
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, clientless, html5, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the only requirement for using a clientless VPN?
**A:** An HTML5-compliant browser
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** vpn, clientless, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a full VPN tunnel?
**A:** All traffic from the client traverses the VPN tunnel — no special forwarding decisions; everything goes through the VPN
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, full-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In a full tunnel, how does third-party website traffic flow?
**A:** Client → VPN tunnel → concentrator decrypts → redirects to internet → website responds → back through concentrator → VPN tunnel → client
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** vpn, full-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a split tunnel VPN?
**A:** A configuration where corporate-bound traffic goes through the VPN tunnel, but non-corporate traffic is sent directly to the internet
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, split-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How many paths does a split tunnel client workstation have?
**A:** Two: 1) VPN tunnel → concentrator → corporate network, and 2) direct internet → third-party websites
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, split-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does the VPN client decide where to route traffic in a split tunnel?
**A:** It recognizes the destination — corporate destinations go through the VPN tunnel; non-corporate destinations are sent directly to the internet
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, split-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the main advantage of split tunnel over full tunnel?
**A:** More efficient for third-party website communication — avoids the extra hop through the concentrator while maintaining secure corporate access
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, split-tunnel, full-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Compare client-to-site, site-to-site, and clientless VPNs.
**A:** Client-to-site: software on workstation to corporate concentrator. Site-to-site: always-on tunnel between firewalls at both locations. Clientless: browser-based via HTML5 Web Cryptography API.
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** vpn, comparison, domain-3, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the disadvantage of a full tunnel when accessing third-party websites?
**A:** All traffic must traverse the VPN tunnel and concentrator even for non-corporate sites, requiring additional routing
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** vpn, full-tunnel, domain-3, prof-messer
