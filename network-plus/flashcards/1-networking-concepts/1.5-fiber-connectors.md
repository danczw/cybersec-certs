# Fiber Connectors Flashcards

**Q:** What does SC stand for in fiber connectors?
**A:** Subscriber Connector
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, sc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are two other names for the SC connector?
**A:** Square connector, standard connector
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, sc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does an SC connector lock into place?
**A:** Push-in with snap lock — pull to unlock and remove
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, sc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why are fiber connectors often combined in pairs?
**A:** One for transmit and one for receive
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does LC stand for in fiber connectors?
**A:** Local Connector (also: Lucent connector, little connector)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, lc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does an LC connector compare in size to an SC?
**A:** LC is slightly smaller than SC
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, lc, sc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does an LC connector lock into place?
**A:** Clip on top locks it in place — push down the clip to release
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, lc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does ST stand for in fiber connectors?
**A:** Straight Tip
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, st, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What lock mechanism does the ST connector use?
**A:** Bayonet connector — push in and twist to lock, reverse twist to unlock
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, st, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What protects the fiber at the end of an ST connector?
**A:** A protective ferrule around the fiber
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, st, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does MPO stand for in fiber connectors?
**A:** Multi-fiber Push On
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, mpo, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How many individual fibers does an MPO connector contain?
**A:** 12
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, mpo, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the advantage of MPO over SC/LC/ST connectors?
**A:** More efficient use of space — 12 fibers in a single connector instead of individual connections
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, mpo, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does an MPO connector lock into place?
**A:** Push-snap lock similar to SC — pull slightly to unlock
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, mpo, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is MTP?
**A:** Corning's brand name for the MPO connector (MTP MPO)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, mpo, mtp, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber connector uses a bayonet (push-twist) lock?
**A:** ST (Straight Tip)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, st, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber connector uses a top clip lock?
**A:** LC (Local Connector)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, lc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber connector uses a push-snap lock?
**A:** SC (Subscriber Connector) — MPO also uses a similar mechanism
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, sc, mpo, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber connector is the smallest: SC, LC, or ST?
**A:** LC (the "little connector")
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, connectors, lc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why do fiber connectors have locking mechanisms?
**A:** To prevent accidental dislodging in crowded racks with many cables
**Difficulty:** Easy
