**Q:** What is rain fade?
**A:** Loss of satellite connectivity during storms due to blocked line of sight

**Tags:** wireless, satellite, domain-1, prof-messer

**Q:** What are the limitations of satellite networking?
**A:** Requires direct line of sight, rain fade during storms, higher cost/complexity, may need backup connectivity
