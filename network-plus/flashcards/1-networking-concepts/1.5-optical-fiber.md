# Optical Fiber Flashcards

**Q:** How does fiber optic cable transmit data?
**A:** Using light (LED or laser)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is fiber optic used on secure networks?
**A:** It is difficult to tap into without detection
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, security, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does tapping difficulty compare between fiber and copper?
**A:** Fiber is very difficult to tap without notification. Copper uses RF and is relatively easy to tap.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, copper, security, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the maximum distance for copper networking vs fiber?
**A:** Copper: ~100 meters. Fiber: up to kilometers (up to 100 km for single-mode).
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, copper, distance, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why might you choose fiber over copper in an environment with interference?
**A:** Fiber is not susceptible to radio frequency interference
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, interference, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the two types of light sources used in fiber optics?
**A:** LED (Light-Emitting Diode) and laser
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name the four components of a fiber optic cable from inside to outside.
**A:** Core, cladding, buffer coating, ferrule (at connector end)
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** fiber, structure, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the core of a fiber optic cable?
**A:** Highly reflective center that carries the light
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, structure, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the cladding in a fiber optic cable?
**A:** Low-reflective layer surrounding the core
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, structure, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the buffer coating in a fiber optic cable?
**A:** Protective outer layer around the cladding
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, structure, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the ferrule on a fiber optic connector?
**A:** Protective cover at the connector end of the fiber
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, structure, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does light travel through a fiber optic cable?
**A:** It bounces through the highly reflective core
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, structure, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is multimode fiber?
**A:** Fiber with a larger core that allows multiple modes (paths) of light to propagate — used for short-range communication
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the maximum distance for multimode fiber?
**A:** Up to ~2 km
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, distance, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What light source does multimode fiber typically use?
**A:** Inexpensive LED
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is it called "multimode" fiber?
**A:** Because the larger core allows multiple modes (paths) of light to bounce through the fiber
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is single-mode fiber?
**A:** Fiber with a smaller core that allows only one mode of light to propagate — used for long-range communication
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, single-mode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the maximum distance for single-mode fiber?
**A:** Up to 100 km without regenerating the signal
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, single-mode, distance, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What light source does single-mode fiber use?
**A:** Intense LED or laser
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, single-mode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why does single-mode fiber use a smaller core than multimode?
**A:** A smaller core only allows a single mode of light to propagate, enabling longer distances
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, single-mode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber type has a larger core — multimode or single-mode?
**A:** Multimode
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, single-mode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber type is used for longer distances — multimode or single-mode?
**A:** Single-mode (up to 100 km vs ~2 km for multimode)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, single-mode, distance, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which fiber type uses a more expensive light source?
**A:** Single-mode (intense LED or laser vs inexpensive LED for multimode)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, multimode, single-mode, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name three advantages of fiber optic over copper cabling.
**A:** Longer distances, not susceptible to RF interference, difficult to tap (more secure)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** fiber, copper, domain-1, prof-messer
