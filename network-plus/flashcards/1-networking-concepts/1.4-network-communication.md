# Network Communication Flashcards

**Q:** What is unicast?
**A:** One-to-one communication — one station sends directly to one other station
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** unicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Give three examples of unicast traffic.
**A:** Web browsing, file transfers, email
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** unicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the main disadvantage of unicast?
**A:** To send the same data to many devices, you must create separate one-to-one sessions for each — inefficient for one-to-many delivery
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** unicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is multicast?
**A:** One-to-many-of-many — one device sends to multiple subscribed recipients simultaneously
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** multicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How do devices receive multicast traffic?
**A:** They subscribe to the multicast feed/group
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** multicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Give three examples of multicast traffic.
**A:** Multimedia streaming, stock exchange data, routing updates
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** multicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why isn't multicast used across different or very large networks?
**A:** It requires network equipment that specifically understands and supports multicast communication
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** multicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is anycast?
**A:** One-to-one-of-many — a single destination IP is routed to the closest of many devices sharing that address
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** anycast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does anycast determine which device receives the traffic?
**A:** The device closest by routing metric receives the traffic
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** anycast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a common use case for anycast?
**A:** Anycast DNS — a DNS query is routed to the nearest data center that can respond
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** anycast, dns, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is broadcast?
**A:** One-to-all — a single packet sent to every device on the local network
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** broadcast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What limits the scope of a broadcast?
**A:** The local broadcast domain — broadcasts cannot cross routers
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** broadcast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Give two examples of broadcast traffic.
**A:** ARP requests, routing updates
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** broadcast, arp, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which IP version removed broadcast? What replaced it?
**A:** IPv6 removed broadcast and replaced it with multicast
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** broadcast, multicast, ipv6, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which communication types work with IPv4 only, IPv6 only, or both?
**A:** Both: unicast, multicast, anycast. IPv4 only: broadcast. IPv6 has no broadcast.
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** unicast, multicast, anycast, broadcast, ipv4, ipv6, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Match: one-to-one, one-to-many-of-many, one-to-one-of-many, one-to-all
**A:** Unicast = one-to-one; Multicast = one-to-many-of-many; Anycast = one-to-one-of-many; Broadcast = one-to-all
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** unicast, multicast, anycast, broadcast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** A company needs to stream a live video to 500 employees on the same network. Unicast or multicast — which is more efficient and why?
**A:** Multicast — sends one stream that all subscribers receive, rather than 500 separate unicast sessions
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** multicast, unicast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why are anycast-receiving devices configured similarly to each other?
**A:** So the originating device gets a consistent response regardless of which device (the closest one) handles the request
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** anycast, communication, domain-1, prof-messer
