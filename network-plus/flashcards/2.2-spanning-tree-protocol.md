# Spanning Tree Protocol Flashcards

**Q:** What causes a loop on a switched network?
**A:** Two cables connected between two switches — frames loop endlessly with no mechanism at the MAC address level to count how many times a frame has been seen
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, loops, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens when a loop occurs on a network?
**A:** Frames loop endlessly, adding more traffic until switches are overwhelmed — can bring down the network in seconds
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, loops, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the manual fix for a network loop?
**A:** Disconnect the cable creating the loop
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, loops, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is Spanning Tree Protocol (STP)?
**A:** An automated standard (IEEE 802.1D) that recognizes and prevents loops on a network
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What IEEE standard defines Spanning Tree Protocol?
**A:** 802.1D
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the five STP port states?
**A:** Blocking, Listening, Learning, Forwarding, Disabled
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** stp, port-states, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does the STP "blocking" port state mean?
**A:** Not forwarding any traffic — used to prevent loops
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, port-states, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does the STP "listening" port state mean?
**A:** Clears existing tables and restarts the process
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, port-states, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does the STP "learning" port state mean?
**A:** Listens to understand switch topology and detect existing loops
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, port-states, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does the STP "forwarding" port state mean?
**A:** Traffic can be transmitted through this interface
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, port-states, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does the STP "disabled" port state mean?
**A:** Administratively disabled — does not participate in STP
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, port-states, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the three STP port roles?
**A:** Root port (RP), Designated port, Blocked port
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, port-roles, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a root port (RP)?
**A:** The interface on a switch connected ultimately to the root bridge
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, port-roles, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a designated port?
**A:** Any other port that can forward traffic but is not the root port
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, port-roles, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a blocked port?
**A:** A port disabled by STP to prevent a loop
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, port-roles, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the root bridge in STP?
**A:** A single switch elected as the reference point for the entire STP topology
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, root-bridge, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How do other switches determine their root ports?
**A:** Based on shortest path to the root bridge
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, root-bridge, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens during an STP topology change (recovery)?
**A:** 1) Link failure detected 2) STP clears existing configuration and relearns topology 3) Blocked ports may become forwarding; root ports may shift 4) Network recovers without creating a loop
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** stp, topology-change, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does RSTP stand for?
**A:** Rapid Spanning Tree Protocol
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, rstp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What IEEE standard defines RSTP?
**A:** 802.1W
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, rstp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How long does convergence take with original STP?
**A:** 30–50 seconds
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How long does convergence take with RSTP?
**A:** ~6 seconds
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, rstp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Is RSTP backwards compatible with original STP?
**A:** Yes — can run alongside original STP on mixed-device networks
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, rstp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does RSTP differ from STP in operation?
**A:** Works very similarly — just operates much faster
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** stp, rstp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens when STP detects a second cable creating a loop?
**A:** STP recognizes the loop and causes one of the interfaces to be in a blocking state
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** stp, loops, domain-2, prof-messer
