# Wireless Networking Flashcards

**Q:** What is an Independent Basic Service Set (IBSS)?
**A:** Direct device-to-device communication without an access point
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, ibss, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is another name for IBSS?
**A:** Ad hoc connection
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, ibss, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a common use case for ad hoc (IBSS) connections?
**A:** Configuring IoT devices (door locks, lights) before connecting them to the main network
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, ibss, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does SSID stand for?
**A:** Service Set Identifier
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, ssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an SSID?
**A:** The name of the wireless network — appears in the list of available networks on client devices
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, ssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Can the same SSID be configured on multiple access points?
**A:** Yes — for coverage across large areas
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, ssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does BSSID stand for?
**A:** Basic Service Set Identifier
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, bssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a BSSID?
**A:** The hardware address of an individual access point
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, bssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the purpose of the BSSID?
**A:** Differentiates between access points that share the same SSID
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, bssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does ESSID stand for?
**A:** Extended Service Set Identifier
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, essid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an ESSID?
**A:** The shared network name used across multiple access points to enable seamless roaming
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, essid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In an ESSID configuration, what do all APs share and what differs between them?
**A:** All share the same SSID; each has a different BSSID (hardware address)
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, essid, bssid, service-sets, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens when a device moves from one AP to another in an ESSID configuration?
**A:** The device moves between APs without notification or interruption (seamless roaming)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, essid, roaming, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a captive portal?
**A:** A screen presented when first connecting to a wireless network, requiring agreement to terms or authentication
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, captive-portal, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What tracks which devices have authenticated through a captive portal?
**A:** A centralized access table
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, captive-portal, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens to unauthenticated devices on a network with a captive portal?
**A:** They are redirected to the captive portal screen
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, captive-portal, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How long does captive portal access typically last?
**A:** A predefined time (e.g., 24 hours), then reauthentication is required
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, captive-portal, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does an open wireless security mode provide?
**A:** No security, no authentication — anyone can communicate freely
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, security, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does OWE (Opportunistic Wireless Encryption) do?
**A:** Encrypts traffic but prevents device-to-device communication
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, security, owe, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is WEP?
**A:** Legacy wireless encryption (insecure)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, security, wep, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the relationship between WPA, WPA2, and WPA3?
**A:** Progressively stronger wireless security standards
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, security, wpa, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does PSK stand for in wireless security?
**A:** Pre-Shared Key
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, security, psk, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is Personal (PSK) wireless security?
**A:** Everyone uses the same password to access the network
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, security, psk, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is Enterprise (802.1X) wireless security?
**A:** Individual username/password authentication; disabling an account removes wireless access
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, security, enterprise, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is Enterprise (802.1X) more secure than PSK for businesses?
**A:** Users authenticate with their own corporate credentials — disabling an account immediately removes access
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, security, enterprise, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an omnidirectional antenna?
**A:** An antenna that distributes signal evenly in all directions
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where are omnidirectional antennas commonly found?
**A:** Consumer/home access points
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When is an omnidirectional antenna ideal?
**A:** When the AP is centrally located
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When is an omnidirectional antenna wasteful?
**A:** When the AP is in a corner — signal goes where no one connects
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a directional antenna do?
**A:** Focuses signal in a single direction
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are two use cases for directional antennas?
**A:** Corner placement and building-to-building links
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is directional antenna performance measured?
**A:** In decibels of gain
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does every 3 dB of gain represent?
**A:** A doubling of power
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a Yagi antenna?
**A:** A directional antenna that is very directional with high gain
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, yagi, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a parabolic antenna?
**A:** A directional antenna that focuses signals into a single feed horn; useful over long distances
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, antennas, parabolic, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an autonomous access point?
**A:** A standalone AP that operates independently with no additional hardware or software required
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where are autonomous access points commonly used?
**A:** Home environments
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a lightweight access point?
**A:** Hardware mounted in ceiling with configuration/intelligence maintained on the switch; less expensive to deploy and managed centrally
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does a lightweight AP differ from an autonomous AP?
**A:** Lightweight AP hardware is separate from its configuration (maintained on the switch); autonomous AP is fully standalone
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does CAPWAP stand for?
**A:** Control and Provisioning of Wireless Access Points
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, capwap, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is CAPWAP?
**A:** A standard for managing wireless access points from one central station
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, capwap, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the central station in CAPWAP called?
**A:** Wireless LAN Controller (WLC)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, capwap, wlc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does "single pane of glass" mean in wireless management?
**A:** Viewing the entire wireless infrastructure from one single screen (the WLC)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, wlc, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are four functions of a wireless LAN controller?
**A:** Deploy new APs, monitor existing AP performance, push configuration changes, generate usage reports
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, wlc, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Are WLCs typically from the same manufacturer as the APs they manage?
**A:** Yes — usually paired with APs from the same manufacturer
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, wlc, ap-management, domain-2, prof-messer
