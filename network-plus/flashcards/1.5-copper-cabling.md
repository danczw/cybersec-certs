# Copper Cabling Flashcards

**Q:** What is the most common cable type for wired Ethernet?
**A:** Twisted pair copper cable
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, twisted-pair, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How are wires arranged inside a twisted pair cable?
**A:** Multiple wires twisted together in a single sheath, paired as transmit+/transmit- and receive+/receive-
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twisted-pair, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is the same signal sent in opposite forms (+ and -) on a twisted pair?
**A:** To detect and correct interference — the receiver compares both signals and cancels out noise that affected both equally
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twisted-pair, interference, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does the twist in twisted pair help with interference?
**A:** One wire is always moving away from the interference source due to the twist, so interference affects both wires equally and can be cancelled
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twisted-pair, interference, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why do different pairs within a twisted pair cable have different twist rates?
**A:** To prevent the pairs from interfering with each other inside the same cable
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twisted-pair, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Does a copper cable have a speed?
**A:** No — the cable has no speed. The Ethernet standard determines the throughput over that cable type.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twisted-pair, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What determines the minimum cable category for an Ethernet standard?
**A:** The IEEE 802.3 standards specify the minimum cable category required
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, categories, ieee, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a cable category?
**A:** A classification standard for twisted pair cables (e.g., Cat 5, Cat 6, Cat 7) that defines minimum performance characteristics
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, categories, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the minimum cable category for 1000BASE-T?
**A:** Category 5
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, categories, 1000base-t, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Can you use a higher category cable than the minimum required?
**A:** Yes — any category at or above the minimum will work
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, categories, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does "coaxial" mean?
**A:** Two or more forms within the cable share a common axis (concentric layers around one center)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, coaxial, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the structure of a coaxial cable from inside to outside?
**A:** Conductor, insulator, shielding, jacket
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, coaxial, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What role does the outer shielding play in coaxial cable?
**A:** It is the second conductor (return path), not just protection
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** copper, coaxial, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is RG-6?
**A:** A common type of coaxial cable used for cable modem / internet connections
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, coaxial, rg-6, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is twinaxial (twinax) cable?
**A:** A coaxial-style cable with two conductors inside, associated with 10 Gbps Ethernet
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twinax, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What speed is twinax commonly associated with?
**A:** 10 Gbps Ethernet
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, twinax, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What interface is twinax often installed as part of?
**A:** SFP+ interface
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twinax, sfp+, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the maximum distance for twinax cable?
**A:** ~5 meters
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twinax, distance, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name two advantages of twinax over other cable types.
**A:** Lower cost than fiber optics, lower latency than twisted pair
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, twinax, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Does twinax support full duplex?
**A:** Yes — full duplex over a single cable
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, twinax, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a plenum?
**A:** The space between a drop ceiling and the actual ceiling — a shared air space
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, plenum, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is the plenum a concern for cabling?
**A:** If there's no ductwork, air flows through the plenum. In a fire, cable smoke and fumes would be distributed throughout the building.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, plenum, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When is plenum-rated cable NOT required?
**A:** When ductwork is used to transfer air — there is no shared plenum in that case
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, plenum, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What material is a standard (non-plenum) cable jacket made of?
**A:** PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, plenum, pvc, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What material is a plenum-rated cable jacket made of?
**A:** FEP (Fluorinated Ethylene Polymer) or low-smoke PVC
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** copper, plenum, fep, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are two properties of plenum-rated cable in a fire?
**A:** Produces less smoke and fewer hazardous fumes
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, plenum, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a disadvantage of plenum-rated cable compared to standard?
**A:** Less flexible — harder to install around twists and turns
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** copper, plenum, domain-1, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What should you check before installing cable in a building?
**A:** Where the cable is being run — use plenum-rated cable in shared air spaces
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** copper, plenum, domain-1, prof-messer
