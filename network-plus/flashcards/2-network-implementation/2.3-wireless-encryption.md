# Wireless Encryption Flashcards

**Q:** What are the three security goals for wireless networks?
**A:** Authentication (limit network access), confidentiality (encryption), and integrity (message integrity check)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, security, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are examples of wireless authentication methods?
**A:** Username, password, or multi-factor authentication
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, authentication, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does confidentiality provide on a wireless network?
**A:** Encryption of all data sent over the wireless network
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does a message integrity check verify?
**A:** That the received data matches exactly what was originally sent
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, integrity, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does WEP stand for?
**A:** Wired Equivalent Privacy
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wep, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why was WEP replaced?
**A:** It has significant cryptographic vulnerabilities
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wep, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does WPA stand for?
**A:** Wi-Fi Protected Access
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What was the purpose of the original WPA?
**A:** A temporary stopgap between insecure WEP and a more secure replacement
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What hardware did the original WPA run on?
**A:** The same hardware as WEP — it was designed to work on existing WEP hardware
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When was WPA2 introduced?
**A:** 2004
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa2, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What block cipher mode does WPA2 use?
**A:** CCMP (Counter Mode with Cipher Block Chaining Message Authentication Code Protocol / Counter/CBC-MAC)
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa2, ccmp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does CCMP stand for?
**A:** Counter Mode with Cipher Block Chaining Message Authentication Code Protocol (Counter/CBC-MAC)
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa2, ccmp, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does CCMP combine into one protocol?
**A:** Encryption and integrity
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa2, ccmp, domain-2, prof-messer

**Q:** What encryption algorithm does WPA2 use?
**A:** AES

**Q:** What encryption algorithm do both WPA2 and WPA3 use for data confidentiality?

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, wpa2, wpa3, domain-2, prof-messer

**Q:** What is the best practice for wireless security?
**A:** Use the highest level of security available on APs and client devices; upgrade all devices to the most capable security method
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, best-practice, domain-2, prof-messer

**Q:** What is the chronological order of wireless encryption standards?
**A:** WEP → WPA → WPA2 → WPA3
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, encryption, domain-2, prof-messer

**Q:** Why is wireless encryption particularly important?
**A:** Wireless networks can be tapped into and listened to by any device nearby
