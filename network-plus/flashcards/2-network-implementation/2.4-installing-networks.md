# Installing Networks Flashcards

**Q:** What is a distribution frame?
**A:** An area where cables are passively terminated using punch down blocks or patch panels
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, distribution-frame, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where is a distribution frame usually located?
**A:** On the back wall of a data center
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, distribution-frame, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What types of cables does a distribution frame terminate?
**A:** LAN and voice communication cables
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, distribution-frame, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does MDF stand for?
**A:** Main Distribution Frame
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, mdf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an MDF?
**A:** A single room — the data center or central point of the network where WAN and internal LAN connections are punched down
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, mdf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is the MDF a perfect testing point?
**A:** Because both internal LAN and external WAN connections are terminated there
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, mdf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does IDF stand for?
**A:** Intermediate Distribution Frame
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, idf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where is an IDF typically located?
**A:** On a separate floor or in a separate building from the MDF
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, idf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What equipment does an IDF typically include?
**A:** Switches, routers, and other equipment needed to connect that area to the MDF
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, idf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How do users connect to the network through an IDF?
**A:** Users on a floor connect to a switch in the IDF, which connects back to the core switch in the MDF
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, idf, mdf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In what size environments are MDF/IDF combinations common?
**A:** Medium to large environments with many remote rooms connecting back to a central data center
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, idf, mdf, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the standard width of a data center rack?
**A:** 19 inches
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a rack unit (U)?
**A:** A standard unit of height measurement for rack equipment — 1U = 1.75 inches
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How tall are most standard racks?
**A:** Approximately 42U
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What rack dimension varies between racks?
**A:** Depth — must match the length of equipment being installed
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why can equipment be stacked in racks?
**A:** Because of the standardized 19-inch width — equipment is designed to fit and stack to maximize space
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What do locked racks provide?
**A:** Additional security and control over components; a door with lock prevents unauthorized access
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, security, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How are locked racks cooled?
**A:** Ventilation in front, top, and bottom for proper airflow
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, racks, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does HVAC stand for?
**A:** Heating, Ventilating, and Air Conditioning
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What three requirements must an HVAC system meet in a data center?
**A:** Support the heat load, have sufficient power, and integrate with the fire system
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a cold aisle?
**A:** The front of servers where cold air is pulled in
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, hot-cold-aisle, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a hot aisle?
**A:** The back of servers where hot air is blown out
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, hot-cold-aisle, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How must equipment be oriented relative to hot and cold aisles?
**A:** Intake (front) faces the cold aisle; exhaust (back) faces the hot aisle
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, hot-cold-aisle, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Describe the airflow cycle in a raised floor data center.
**A:** HVAC sends cold air under raised floor → vents let cold air into cold aisle → servers pull in cold air from front → heated air exits back into hot aisle → hot air rises to ceiling → HVAC pulls it back in and re-cools
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, hot-cold-aisle, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How do data centers without raised floors handle cooling?
**A:** Cold air blown directly into cold aisle from above; cold aisles may be covered with plastic to contain cold air; hot air returns to ceiling and back to HVAC
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** infrastructure, hvac, hot-cold-aisle, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is on the back side of a patch panel?
**A:** A 110 block where wires are punched down
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is on the front side of a patch panel?
**A:** RJ45 modular connectors
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What connects from the patch panel to the switch?
**A:** Patch cables from the RJ45 connectors on the patch panel to switch ports
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Once cables are installed from a patch panel to desks, are they ever moved?
**A:** No — installed cables from patch panel to desks are never moved once installed
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How are patch panel connections identified?
**A:** Numbered connections that identify which desk each port serves
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is a "move" handled with patch panels?
**A:** Relocate the patch cable to a different connection in the IDF
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, mac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is an "add" handled with patch panels?
**A:** Connect new employees without running new cables — cables are already run to desks; just add a patch cable in the IDF
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, mac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** For moves, adds, and changes, what is the only thing that needs to change?
**A:** Internal connections inside the IDF (patch cables between patch panel and switch)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, mac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a fiber optic distribution panel?
**A:** A panel that brings in fiber runs from other buildings or floors and provides access to those connections
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, fiber, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What must not be exceeded when routing fiber in a distribution panel?
**A:** The bend radius
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, fiber, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is proper bend radius maintained inside a fiber distribution panel?
**A:** Large loops of fiber inside the panel
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, fiber, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a service loop in fiber optics?
**A:** Extra fiber wrapped around inside the panel for future flexibility — allows extending or moving without rerunning fiber
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** infrastructure, fiber, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where do single cable runs go from each desk?
**A:** Back to the IDF
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** infrastructure, patch-panel, domain-2, prof-messer
