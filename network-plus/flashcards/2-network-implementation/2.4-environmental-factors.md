# Environmental Factors Flashcards

**Q:** What percentage of all US power consumption is used by data centers?
**A:** Approximately 9%
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, power, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does much of data center power go toward?
**A:** Managing the environment — humidity, temperature, and other controls
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, power, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What problem does high humidity cause in a data center?
**A:** Condensation (water in the data center)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, humidity, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What problem does low humidity cause in a data center?
**A:** Excessive static discharge, which is harmful to equipment
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, humidity, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the ideal humidity range for a data center?
**A:** 40%–60%
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, humidity, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is 40%–60% the ideal humidity range?
**A:** High enough to prevent static discharge, low enough to prevent condensation
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, humidity, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the optimal temperature range for a data center?
**A:** 64–81°F
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, temperature, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why do data centers need cooling?
**A:** Equipment generates heat as it runs, and that heat must be cooled
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, temperature, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What two external influences can affect data center temperature?
**A:** Outdoor temperature changes (add load to cooling) and increased system load (creates more heat)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, temperature, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the role of HVAC in a data center?
**A:** Maintain specific humidity and temperature levels across the entire facility
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, hvac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does HVAC ensure consistent conditions throughout a data center?
**A:** Sensors located throughout the data center monitor temperature and humidity
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, hvac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is water not suitable for fire suppression in a data center?
**A:** Data centers contain large amounts of electrical equipment
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, fire-suppression, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What do data centers use for fire suppression instead of water?
**A:** Inert gas or chemical agent stored in a tank
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, fire-suppression, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does the fire suppression agent do?
**A:** Suppresses fire or removes oxygen from the air
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, fire-suppression, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens when a fire alarm is pulled in a data center?
**A:** Chemicals are dispersed throughout the entire data center
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, fire-suppression, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Can fire suppression chemicals damage equipment?
**A:** Yes — may cause damage but prevents fire from spreading
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** environment, fire-suppression, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is fire suppression integrated with HVAC?
**A:** Fire detection shuts down HVAC to prevent oxygen from feeding the fire
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** environment, fire-suppression, hvac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens to the HVAC when a fire is detected?
**A:** It shuts down to prevent oxygen from feeding the fire
