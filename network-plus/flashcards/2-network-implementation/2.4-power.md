# Power Flashcards

**Q:** Why must you disconnect a device from power before working on it?
**A:** To avoid contact with voltage — never put yourself in a position to contact any energized component
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, safety, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why can some devices shock you even when disconnected from power?
**A:** Some devices store charge in capacitors (CRT monitors, laser printers)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, safety, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an ampere (amp)?
**A:** The rate of electrons moving past a point per second
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In the water hose analogy, what does amperage correspond to?
**A:** Hose diameter
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is voltage?
**A:** The pressure of electricity flowing through a wire
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In the water hose analogy, what does voltage correspond to?
**A:** Water pressure
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a watt?
**A:** A measure of power being used — calculated as volts × amps
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How do you calculate watts?
**A:** Volts × amps (e.g., 120V × 0.5A = 60W)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** In the water hose analogy, what do watts correspond to?
**A:** Total water flow
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, electrical-terms, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is Alternating Current (AC)?
**A:** Current that changes direction as it moves through wire — represented by a wavy line
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why is AC used for homes and offices?
**A:** It's easy to distribute over long distances
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, ac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the voltage and frequency specs for AC in the US/Canada?
**A:** 110–120V at 60 Hz
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the voltage and frequency specs for AC in Europe?
**A:** 220–240V at 50 Hz
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ac, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is Direct Current (DC)?
**A:** Current that moves in a single direction with constant voltage
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, dc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What symbol represents DC?
**A:** A solid line on top with dashed lines below
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** power, dc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why do networking devices need power supplies?
**A:** To convert AC (from wall outlet) to DC (which electrical components use to operate)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ac, dc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where can AC-to-DC conversion occur?
**A:** In power supplies built into the device, or in the power cord
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ac, dc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does UPS stand for?
**A:** Uninterruptible Power Supply
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What three power problems does a UPS protect against?
**A:** Power outages, brownouts (voltage drops), and surges (voltage spikes)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a brownout?
**A:** A drop in voltage
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a surge?
**A:** A spike in voltage (voltage goes much higher)
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an offline/standby UPS?
**A:** Activates after power loss; small gap with no power may reboot devices
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the drawback of an offline/standby UPS?
**A:** Small gap with no power when switching — often enough to reboot switches/routers
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is a line-interactive UPS?
**A:** Compensates for brownouts by increasing power when voltage drops
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an online/double-conversion UPS?
**A:** Always runs from battery; batteries constantly refreshed by power source; losing power has no effect on systems
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which UPS type is preferred in data centers?
**A:** Online/double-conversion — systems always run from battery so power loss has no effect
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name four features that vary between UPS models.
**A:** Automatic shutdown on low battery, battery capacity, number of outlets, additional interfaces (phone lines, cable modem)
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** power, ups, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does PDU stand for?
**A:** Power Distribution Unit
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, pdu, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does a PDU differ from a normal surge suppressor?
**A:** It's an intelligent device that allows remote connection and management of each interface
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, pdu, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What can you do remotely with a PDU?
**A:** Connect to the device, manage each interface, and power off/on devices from a remote location
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, pdu, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How is a PDU managed over the network?
**A:** It has an ethernet connection with an IP address and built-in web server
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** power, pdu, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Is a PDU rack-mountable?
**A:** Yes
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** power, pdu, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**A:** Online/double-conversion — it's always running from battery
