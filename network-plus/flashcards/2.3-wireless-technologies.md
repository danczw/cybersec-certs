**Q:** What is band steering?
**A:** An AP feature that controls which frequency band a dual-band (or multi-band) client uses when connecting
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, band-steering, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Without band steering, how does a device choose which frequency to connect on?
**A:** It connects to whichever frequency has the strongest signal
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, band-steering, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why doesn't the strongest signal always provide the best throughput?
**A:** A different frequency band may offer better throughput even if its signal is weaker — signal strength and throughput are not the same
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, band-steering, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Give an example of band steering in action.
**A:** A client that supports both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz is steered to prefer 5 GHz by the AP administrator
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, band-steering, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Who is the administrator that configures band steering?
**A:** The access point administrator — band steering is configured on the AP, not the client
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** wireless, band-steering, domain-2, prof-messer

**Q:** What is Dynamic Frequency Selection (DFS)?
**A:** A feature that allows APs to automatically select channels that won't conflict with other access points already operating
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, dfs, regulatory, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What happens when the wireless environment changes and an AP using DFS needs a new channel?
**A:** The AP changes channels and tells all connected clients to move with it
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, dfs, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Why don't modern APs require manual channel configuration?
**A:** DFS automatically detects frequencies in use and selects non-conflicting channels
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, dfs, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is Transmit Power Control (TPC)?
**A:** A feature that puts transmit power control in the hands of the access point — the AP tells clients what power level to use
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, tpc, regulatory, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How does TPC differ from traditional power configuration?
**A:** Traditionally, clients set their own transmit power; with TPC, the AP controls client transmit power
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, tpc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What benefit does TPC provide?
**A:** Many devices can communicate at optimal power levels without conflicting with other services
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** wireless, tpc, domain-2, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What guidelines does 802.11h follow?
**A:** Guidelines from the International Telecommunications Union (ITU)
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** wireless, regulatory, domain-2, prof-messer

**Q:** What two interoperability features are defined in 802.11h?
