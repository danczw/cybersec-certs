# Regulatory Compliance Flashcards

**Q:** What is compliance in IT?
**A:** Maintaining adherence to laws, policies, procedures, or other rules associated with a type of work
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** compliance, regulatory, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name three possible consequences of non-compliance.
**A:** Fines, incarceration, or loss of employment
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** compliance, regulatory, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What are the possible scopes of compliance requirements?
**A:** National, territory, state, domestic, or international
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** compliance, regulatory, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What drives compliance requirements?
**A:** Some are driven by the type of business; others by state, local, or international laws
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** compliance, regulatory, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is data localization?
**A:** The requirement that data collected by a country must stay inside that country
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** data-localization, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

**Q:** What does GDPR stand for?
**A:** General Data Protection Regulation
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** gdpr, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which geographic region is the GDPR associated with?
**A:** The European Union
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** gdpr, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the primary purpose of the GDPR?
**A:** Protecting the privacy of data associated with individuals residing in the European Union
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** gdpr, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name at least five types of data protected under the GDPR.
**A:** Name, address, photo, email address, bank information, websites visited
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** gdpr, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Where must data collected on EU citizens be stored according to GDPR?
**A:** In the European Union
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** gdpr, data-localization, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What rights do users have over their data under the GDPR?
**A:** They can decide where their data goes and can choose to have their data removed from sites
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** gdpr, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** The GDPR is often described as a "right to be forgotten." What is a better description?
**A:** A way for individuals to protect and control data that they themselves own
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** gdpr, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What does PCI DSS stand for?
**A:** Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Is PCI DSS a law or a standard?
**A:** A standard — created by the payment card industry, not a government law
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is PCI DSS designed to protect?
**A:** Credit card information
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the consequence for an organization that fails a PCI DSS audit?
**A:** They may lose the ability to process credit cards
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How many areas of focus does PCI DSS have?
**A:** Six
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name the six areas of focus in PCI DSS.
**A:** 1) Build/maintain secure networks and systems, 2) Protect cardholder data, 3) Maintain a vulnerability management program, 4) Implement strong access control measures, 5) Regularly monitor and test networks, 6) Maintain an information security policy
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which PCI DSS focus area ensures only authorized personnel can access credit card data?
**A:** Implement strong access control measures
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which PCI DSS focus area ensures policies are working as expected?
**A:** Regularly monitor and test networks
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which PCI DSS focus area is especially critical for organizations storing credit card data?
**A:** Maintain a vulnerability management program
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Which PCI DSS focus area provides the broadest scope of protection across all organizational data?
**A:** Maintain an information security policy
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the difference between GDPR and PCI DSS in terms of enforcement?
**A:** GDPR is a government regulation (EU law) with legal penalties; PCI DSS is an industry standard enforced through audits with the penalty of losing credit card processing ability
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Tags:** gdpr, pci-dss, compliance, domain-4, prof-messer
