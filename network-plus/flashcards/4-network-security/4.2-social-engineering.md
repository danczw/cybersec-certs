# Social Engineering Flashcards

**Q:** What is phishing?
**A:** Social engineering with spoofing — pretending to be someone else to trick victims into giving up personal information
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** phishing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name five indicators of a phishing email.
**A:** URL doesn't match expected domain, graphics slightly off, spelling/grammar errors, mismatched fonts, sender's email domain doesn't match the claimed organization
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** phishing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the attacker's goal with a phishing page?
**A:** Trick the victim into entering credentials on a fake page, sending them directly to the attacker
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** phishing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the best practice regarding links in email messages?
**A:** Never click links inside email messages
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** phishing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is shoulder surfing?
**A:** Viewing someone's screen in a public location such as an airport, restaurant, or coffee shop
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** shoulder-surfing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How can shoulder surfing be performed from a distance?
**A:** Using binoculars or a telescope from another building to read a screen
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** shoulder-surfing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the advanced/remote version of shoulder surfing?
**A:** Malware that enables the victim's camera to watch them remotely
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** shoulder-surfing, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name four mitigations for shoulder surfing.
**A:** Be aware of surroundings (sit with back to wall), avoid viewing sensitive info in public, use privacy filters on LCD screens, position monitors away from windows
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** shoulder-surfing, mitigation, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** How do privacy filters prevent shoulder surfing?
**A:** They make the display appear black from side angles — only the person directly in front can see the screen content
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** shoulder-surfing, mitigation, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is tailgating?
**A:** Following an authorized person through a secured door without their knowledge — e.g., catching a door before it closes after someone badges in
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** tailgating, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is piggybacking?
**A:** An authorized person knowingly lets an unauthorized person in — e.g., asking someone to hold the door while carrying donuts or lunch
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** piggybacking, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is the difference between tailgating and piggybacking?
**A:** Tailgating: the authorized person doesn't know you're following them in. Piggybacking: the authorized person knowingly helps you get in.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** tailgating, piggybacking, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name four mitigations for tailgating and piggybacking.
**A:** Visitor badge policy, challenge people without badges, one-person-at-a-time badging, access control vestibule (airlock)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** tailgating, piggybacking, mitigation, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is an access control vestibule?
**A:** A mechanical airlock that allows only one person into a building at a time — enforces one-person-at-a-time access physically rather than by policy
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** tailgating, piggybacking, mitigation, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What is dumpster diving?
**A:** Going through an organization's trash to find sensitive information
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** dumpster-diving, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What types of information can be found through dumpster diving?
**A:** Names (for impersonation/phishing), contact information, and project details
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** dumpster-diving, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** When is the best timing for dumpster diving?
**A:** End of a quarter or end of a big project — when a lot of information may be thrown out
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** dumpster-diving, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Is dumpster diving legal in the United States?
**A:** Generally legal if trash has been thrown out, unless it's on private property with no-trespassing signs or local/state regulations prohibit it
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** dumpster-diving, social-engineering, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** Name four mitigations for dumpster diving.
**A:** Lock up garbage bins, fence around the area, monitoring cameras, shred sensitive information
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Tags:** dumpster-diving, mitigation, domain-4, prof-messer

---

**Q:** What extreme method do governments use to prevent information recovery from trash?
**A:** Burning sensitive documents — once burned, no one can recover the information
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Tags:** dumpster-diving, mitigation, domain-4, prof-messer
