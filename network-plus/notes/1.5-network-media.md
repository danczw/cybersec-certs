# Network Media (Objective 1.5)

## Copper Cabling

| Category | Speed | Max Distance | Use |
|----------|-------|-------------|-----|
| Cat 5e | 1 Gbps | 100m | Legacy networks |
| Cat 6 | 10 Gbps (55m) / 1 Gbps (100m) | 55-100m | Enterprise |
| Cat 6a | 10 Gbps | 100m | Enterprise (shielded) |
| Cat 7 | 10 Gbps | 100m | Data centers (fully shielded) |
| Cat 8 | 25-40 Gbps | 30m | Data center switch-to-switch |

## Fiber Optic

| Type | Core Size | Distance | Use |
|------|-----------|----------|-----|
| Single-mode (SMF) | 8-10 μm | Up to 80+ km | Long-haul, WAN |
| Multimode (MMF) | 50/62.5 μm | Up to 2 km | Short-haul, LAN |

## Connectors

### Fiber Connectors
- **LC** — small form-factor, most common in enterprise
- **SC** — square/snap-in, older standard
- **ST** — bayonet-style, round
- **MPO** — multi-fiber (12/24 fibers), data centers

### Copper Connectors
- **RJ-45** — Ethernet (8-pin)
- **RJ-11** — Phone/DSL (6-pin)
- **F-type** — Coaxial (cable TV, DOCSIS)
- **BNC** — Coaxial (legacy Ethernet, video)

## Transceivers

| Type | Speed | Use |
|------|-------|-----|

| 10GBASE-T | 10 Gbps | Cat 6a copper |

| 10GBASE-LR | 10 Gbps | Single-mode fiber |
