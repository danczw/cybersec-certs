# Segmentation Enforcement

## Network Segmentation Overview

- Physical separation of devices or logical/virtual segmentation (e.g., VLANs)
- Purposes:
  - **Performance** — isolate high-bandwidth applications onto their own network
  - **Security** — limit which devices can communicate with each other
  - **Compliance** — some regulations (e.g., PCI DSS) require segmentation for storing credit card data

## IoT (Internet of Things) Segmentation

- IoT devices: sensors (cooling, heating, lighting), smart devices (home automation, video doorbells, cameras), wearables (smartwatch, health monitor)
- Problem: IoT devices may be ingenious in operation but poorly designed for security
- Solution: segment IoT devices onto their own network, away from production data

## IIoT (Industrial Internet of Things)

- Machine-to-machine communication to keep industrial systems running
- Higher stakes than consumer IoT — entire manufacturing lines depend on proper communication
- Segmentation prevents external devices from disrupting critical communication
- Use cases: oil and gas systems, medical devices in hospitals
- Critical when lives are on the line

## SCADA / ICS

- SCADA: Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition
- ICS: Industrial Control Systems
- Large industrial systems: manufacturing environments, power generation
- Require real-time monitoring and immediate network control
- Completely segmented from the rest of the network
- Only people with the right access in the right location can access these systems

## Operational Technology (OT)

- Systems designed for critical infrastructure: electric grid, traffic control, manufacturing plants
- Uptime and availability are paramount
- Failure consequences:
  - Traffic lights malfunction or stop working
  - Manufacturing plants shut down completely
  - Power generation/distribution disrupted
- Network segmentation creates a stronger security posture for OT

## Guest Networks

- Separate wireless network for visitors
- Allows internet access but prevents access to internal services
- Access controlled by passphrase or login authentication
- Segmentation keeps guest traffic isolated from internal network

## BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)

- Personal mobile device used for work purposes (also called "bring your own technology")
- Device must meet organization's security requirements
- Segmentation separates personal data from company data on the same device
- Benefits:
  - Private information stays private
  - Company data managed by system administrator
  - If employee leaves: company data removed, personal data remains
